## Context

I built the original site and implemented its redesign, organizing six practice areas, the attorney’s background, and consultation options. Visitors can call directly or complete a guided form that opens a draft in their own email app, where they must press Send. The firm manages availability and follow-up outside the website.

My work covered React Router implementation, prerendered public content, centralized firm information, mobile navigation, release checks, and hosting security headers.
